SAT-Insight-Gen — generation architecture

BUILD_SPEC four-agent linear pipeline + deterministic two-tier selector · branch opus-double-gate · 2026-06-21
Model-first authoring: the harness pins the difficulty-fixing radicals + a structure directive (not exact numbers); the author invents a natural situation and chooses fitting numbers; PROVE re-derives, the LLTM gates the band. Graph tasks stay harness-pinned (figure + answer); structure-analysis & parallel/perpendicular tasks are forced abstract (the real SAT poses them with no scenario).
Plausibility gate (new): on context items, an LLM checks objective real-world coherence only — implausible quantities, broken physics, wrong answer-type. Novelty is explicitly NOT a defect; we do not optimize toward resembling memorized released items (that would be pre-training-target hitting).
Double gate: any gate failure becomes a concrete revision instruction → re-author → re-gate; dropped only after the repair budget is spent.
