SAT-Insight-Gen — four-agent generator (shared engine · per-domain distinct)

One linear pipeline parameterized by domain. What defines a domain is distinct & isolated; the orchestration, verifiers, difficulty model, dedup, and render are shared.

Reading the diagram. Blue = the only things that change per domain (cell schema, drawers, construct-backward generators, voice anchors). Teal = shared engine reused unchanged. Orange = the gates that carry all trust — nothing the producer emits is kept until it is independently re-derived (PROVE), measured (judge), and shown to be a distinct structure (dedup). Novelty is the goal: new structures, not reskins, measured by distinct fingerprints — the absolute judge is a defect detector, never a recognition target.
