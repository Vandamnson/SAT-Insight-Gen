# Geometry & Trig difficulty calibration guide (blind expert calibration, 2026-07-01)

_Produced by a context-free expert agent given ONLY the Skills-Insight GEO rubric + RP67; adopted as the
operative banding instrument for geometry until real CB geometry anchors exist (judge has 0 GEO anchors)._

**Principle: an item's band is set by its HARDEST GATE — usually recall/recognition, not arithmetic.**
Gates by severity: (1) formula on reference sheet → cheap · (2) famous-but-off-sheet fact (Pythagorean,
angle sum) → moderate · (3) off-sheet theorem that must be RECALLED, or a relationship that must be
RECOGNIZED from the configuration → expensive. Inverse ≈ +1 band over forward; chain ≈ +1 over hardest link.

| Band | Operational features |
|---|---|
| B1 (<370) | One step, forward, sheet formula, all quantities labeled, small integers, no π. |
| B2 (370–410) | One FACT (not sheet formula): perimeter, vertical/parallel-line angles; one add/subtract setup. |
| B3 (420–460) | One famous relationship FORWARD with one embedded op: Pythag hypotenuse; angle-sum solve; sheet formula needing a square or symbolic π (kπ). |
| B4 (470–540) | One relationship + one extra demand: fractional factor (⅓ pyramid), INVERSE Pythagorean, special right triangles, plain similarity ratio. |
| B5 (550–600) | Two familiar links chained; OR one-step inverse on a sheet formula (volume→dimension); OR drilled off-sheet identity (sin/cos complement, radians). |
| B6 (610–670) | 2–3-link chain where ≥1 link must be RECOGNIZED (auxiliary segment, right trig ratio, scale² /scale³); inverse on multi-term formulas; unfamiliar orientation. |
| B7 (680–800) | Gated by an OFF-SHEET THEOREM recalled outright — inscribed angle, arc/sector proportionality, intersecting chords, tangent properties WHEN NOT STATED, geometric-mean similarity — or multi-link trig/similarity chains. Clean arithmetic does not rescue these. |

**Generator lessons adopted:**
- Stating the off-sheet theorem in the stem COLLAPSES difficulty (a tangent item whose stem gives PT⊥OT is
  inverse-Pythagorean B4, not circle-theorem B7). For B7 circle items, do not state the theorem.
- Statement-bundle mismatches: cylinder forward volume is B3/B4 in practice even though the cells file maps
  it under the B6 prism statement — the statement text bundles harder (surface-area/dimension) cases.
