# Digital SAT Math — Geometry & Trigonometry: Skills-Insight performance bands

Bands correspond to SAT Math section scale scores: B1 = scale below 370 · B2 = 370–410 · B3 = 420–460 · B4 = 470–540 · B5 = 550–600 · B6 = 610–670 · B7 = 680–800.

Item placement rule (RP67): an item belongs to the LOWEST band whose students have at least a 0.67 probability of answering it correctly. Students in bands below an item's band answer it correctly less often than that.

What students at each band can typically do:

## Band 1
- Find the volume of a right rectangular prism when given the lengths of the edges and the formula for the volume.
- Find the area of a rectangle when given the lengths of the sides.

## Band 2
- Solve problems involving the perimeter and side lengths of plane figures.
- Solve problems by applying theorems related to parallel lines cut by a transversal.

## Band 3
- Solve problems involving the area and side lengths of plane figures.
- Find the measure of an angle by applying definitions and theorems about angles, such as the triangle angle sum theorem and theorems related to angles formed by intersecting lines.
- Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of a hypotenuse in a right triangle when given the lengths of the two legs.

## Band 4
- Solve problems involving the area of a plane figure or the volume of a cube or pyramid.
- Solve problems using concepts and theorems related to scale factors, the sum of angles of triangles, and congruence and similarity.
- Find a side length in a given triangle by applying the Pythagorean theorem; find an angle measure in or a side length of a given triangle using the properties of special right triangles.

## Band 5
- Solve problems using the relationship between sine and cosine of complementary angles; convert between degree measure and radian measure.
- Write an equation of a circle in the xy-plane when given the center of the circle and a point that lies on the circle.
- Solve multistep problems involving area and perimeter of plane figures.

## Band 6
- Calculate the surface area, the volume, or a dimension of a prism when given other information about the prism.
- Solve complex problems by applying the Pythagorean theorem to find a side length of a rectangle or by applying the triangle angle sum theorem.
- Solve complex problems by using concepts and theorems related to congruence and similarity of right triangles, including trigonometric ratios of right triangles, or by identifying the impact of changes by a scale factor on perimeter, area, and volume.

## Band 7
- Solve for missing values in objects modeled by various 2D and 3D geometric shapes by applying formulas for area, surface area, or volume.
- Solve complex problems by applying properties of similar and congruent triangles, theorems related to angles and triangles, or right triangle trigonometry, or use similarity to calculate values of trigonometric ratios.
- Solve problems using properties and theorems related to circles and parts of circles, such as radii, diameters, tangents, angles, arcs, arc length, and sectors.

Note: the digital SAT provides a reference sheet in every module with standard area/volume formulas (rectangle, triangle, circle, prism V=lwh, cylinder, sphere, cone, pyramid) and special-right-triangle facts.
