Band B1Multiple choiceVolume · rectangular prism

What is the volume, in cubic centimeters, of the right rectangular prism shown?

  1. A16
  2. B40
  3. C120
  4. D158
Answer & solution

Answer: C (120)

V = lwh = 8 · 5 · 3 = 120 cubic centimeters.

Distractors: A adds the three edges (8+5+3); B multiplies only two dimensions (8·5); D is the surface area, 2(40+24+15) = 158.

RP67 · B1: one forward substitution into the reference-sheet formula V = lwh — no derived quantity, no inverse step.

In the figure, line ℓ is parallel to line m. What is the value of x?

  1. A28
  2. B62
  3. C118
  4. D152
Answer & solution

Answer: B (62)

The 118° angle and the angle marked x° are same-side interior angles between the parallel lines, so they are supplementary: x = 180 − 118 = 62.

Distractors: A subtracts from 90 instead of 180 (118 − 90); C assumes the two marked angles are congruent; D adds a right angle (62 + 90).

RP67 · B2: a single parallel-lines theorem (same-side interior ⇒ supplementary) followed by one subtraction — the explicit B2 descriptor.

Band B4Multiple choiceSimilarity · parallel segment

In triangle ABC shown, DE is parallel to BC. What is the length of BC?

  1. A5.4
  2. B6
  3. C13
  4. D15
Answer & solution

Answer: D (15)

Since DE ∥ BC, triangle ADE is similar to triangle ABC (AA). AB = 6 + 4 = 10, so the scale factor from ADE to ABC is 10/6, and BC = 9 · (10/6) = 15.

Distractors: A inverts the scale factor, 9 · (6/10); B scales by DB/AD, 9 · (4/6); C adds 4 to 9 instead of scaling.

RP67 · B4: similarity proportion gated on assembling the composite side AB = AD + DB and choosing the correct correspondence — a two-step forward chain.

The figure shows the floor plan of a storage room. All angles of the floor are right angles. What is the area, in square meters, of the floor?

Enter your answer
Answer & solution

Answer: 92

The two unlabeled sides are 14 − 6 = 8 m and 10 − 4 = 6 m. The floor is the 14 × 10 rectangle minus the 8 × 6 notch: 140 − 48 = 92 square meters. (Equivalently, 14 · 4 + 6 · 6 = 56 + 36 = 92.)

RP67 · B5: multistep area — two unlabeled sides must first be derived from the figure, then the region decomposed; a three-step forward chain on sheet formulas.

Band B6Multiple choiceRight-triangle trigonometry

In right triangle ABC shown, what is the value of cos A?

  1. A35
  2. B45
  3. C54
  4. D43
Answer & solution

Answer: B (4/5)

AB = √(16² + 12²) = √400 = 20. Then cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse = AC/AB = 16/20 = 4/5.

Distractors: A is sin A (12/20); C flips the correct ratio (hypotenuse over adjacent); D is tan B (16/12), the tangent of the wrong acute angle.

RP67 · B6: off-sheet SOH-CAH-TOA recall gated behind a Pythagorean step — the hypotenuse is not given, so the ratio sits at the end of a two-link chain.

Band B6Multiple choicePrism · volume → surface area

The volume of the right rectangular prism shown is 336 cubic inches. What is the surface area, in square inches, of the prism?

  1. A146
  2. B196
  3. C244
  4. D292
Answer & solution

Answer: D (292)

Height: h = 336 ÷ (8 · 6) = 7. Surface area = 2(8·6 + 8·7 + 6·7) = 2(48 + 56 + 42) = 2(146) = 292 square inches.

Distractors: A forgets to double the three distinct faces (146); B is the lateral area only, 2(56 + 42); C omits one 8 × 6 base (292 − 48).

RP67 · B6: an inverse step (recover h from V = lwh) chained into an off-sheet surface-area assembly of three face pairs — the explicit B6 prism descriptor.

The circle shown has center O. The length of minor arc AB is 4π centimeters. What is the radius, in centimeters, of the circle?

Enter your answer
Answer & solution

Answer: 18

Arc length = (central angle / 360) · circumference: 4π = (40/360) · 2πr = 2πr/9. So 2r/9 = 4 and r = 18.

RP67 · B7: inverse circle-parts problem — the radius must be recovered from an arc length through the off-sheet central-angle proportion; circles-and-arcs is the explicit B7 descriptor.

Band B7Multiple choiceSimilarity → trig ratio

In the figure, triangle DEF is similar to triangle ABC, where D, E, and F correspond to A, B, and C, respectively. What is the value of tan D?

  1. A2129
  2. B2021
  3. C2120
  4. D2921
Answer & solution

Answer: B (20/21)

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles, so tan D = tan A. In triangle ABC, the side opposite A is BC = 20 and the leg adjacent to A is AC = 21, so tan D = 20/21.

Distractors: A is cos A (21/29); C is tan E, the other acute angle (21/20); D misassembles hypotenuse over adjacent (29/21).

RP67 · B7: transferring a trig ratio across a similarity correspondence — the explicit B7 descriptor "use similarity to calculate values of trigonometric ratios"; nothing about DEF is measured directly.

BAND 7
Band B7Student-produced responsePyramid · total surface area

The solid shown is a right pyramid with a square base. What is the total surface area, in square centimeters, of the pyramid?

Enter your answer
Answer & solution

Answer: 384

The apothem of the base is 12/2 = 6, so the slant height is √(6² + 8²) = 10. Each lateral face has area ½ · 12 · 10 = 60, so the lateral area is 4 · 60 = 240. Adding the base: 240 + 12² = 240 + 144 = 384 square centimeters.

RP67 · B7: the SA target is off-sheet and hidden behind an internal Pythagorean step (apothem → slant height), then a four-face-plus-base assembly — a four-step chain with a non-obvious auxiliary length.
