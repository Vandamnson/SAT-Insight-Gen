Subtract h/2 from both sides and subtract 5 from both sides simultaneously to collect variable terms on the left and constants on the right: 3h − h/2 = 20 − 5. 2. divide coefficient: Divide both sides by the net fractional coefficient 5/2, which is equivalent to multiplying both sides by 2/5: h = 15 × (2/5) = 30/5 = 6.
