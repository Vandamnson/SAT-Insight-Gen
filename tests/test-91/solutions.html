2. divide coefficient: Divide both sides of 3h = 15 by 3: h = 15 ÷ 3 = 5.

3

1. isolate variable: Translate the geological context into an equation: the upper-layer thickness n plus the lower-layer thickness 16/2 equals the total thickness 20, giving n + 16/2 = 20.
2. simplify: Simplify the fraction appearing in the equation: 16/2 = 8, so the equation becomes n + 8 = 20. No fraction arithmetic carries forward.
3. subtract constant: Subtract 8 from both sides of n + 8 = 20: n = 20 − 8 = 12.
4. divide coefficient: The coefficient of n is 1; dividing both sides by 1 is trivial and confirms n = 12.

4

1. isolate variable: Translate the word problem into an equation. Nine batches of h gallons on the left; 40 gallons more than one batch on the right: 9h = h + 40.
2. move variable term: Subtract h from both sides to consolidate variable terms: 9h − h = h + 40 − h, giving 8h = 40.
3. divide coefficient: Divide both sides by the coefficient 8: h = 40 ÷ 8 = 5.

5

1. add constant: Add 1 to both sides of 12h − 1 = 34 to isolate the variable term: 12h − 1 + 1 = 34 + 1, yielding 12h = 35.
2. divide coefficient: Divide both sides by 12 to solve for h: h = 35 ÷ 12 = 35/12. Since gcd(35, 12) = 1, the fraction is already in simplest form. The equivalent decimal 2.91̄6̄ may also be entered if the SPR rubric accepts non-terminating decimals.

6

1. isolate variable: Write the equation as given: 2m + 13 = −16.
2. subtract constant: Subtract 13 from both sides: 2m = −16 − 13 = −29.
3. divide coefficient: Divide both sides by 2: m = −29 ÷ 2 = −29/2.
4. simplify: Confirm −29/2 is fully reduced: gcd(29, 2) = 1, so no further simplification is possible. Record −29/2 in the SPR grid.

Answer: -29/2
